Pocono Venues to Know

Two Pocono venues known for weekend-style celebrations, with planning notes.

The Stroudsmoor

Stroudsburg

  • A 200-acre mountaintop estate in the Poconos
  • Three distinct wedding venues: Terraview, Ridgecrest and Woodsgate
  • Part of the Wedgewood Weddings family, with up to 350 guests

Tips for a Poconos Wedding

  • Send travel details early: driving directions, transit options and room blocks help out-of-town guests plan.
  • Plan the whole weekend, not just the day: a welcome gathering, the rehearsal dinner and a farewell brunch each need their own timeline.
  • Mountain weather can change quickly, so a solid backup plan for outdoor moments matters.
  • Ask every venue what’s included and what you bring in (catering, rentals, bar, decor), plus its rules on outside vendors, candles and end times.
  • Have a rain plan for any outdoor ceremony, and agree on when the weather call will be made.
  • Build buffer time into the timeline for photos, travel between ceremony and reception, and hair and makeup.
  • Share one master timeline and vendor contact sheet with every vendor in the final weeks.
